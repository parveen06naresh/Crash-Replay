## 2. Add it to the Projects section

Profile > **Add profile section** > Recommended > **Add projects**.

- **Project name:** Crash Replay: Market-Crash Simulator with a Statistical Skill Test
- **Associated with:** University of South Florida
- **Start date:** Oct 2026 (check "I am currently working on this project")
- **Skills:** Quantitative Finance, Statistics, JavaScript, Python, Data Analysis
- **Media:** link to the game, link to the GitHub repo, and upload `crash-replay-carousel.pdf`
  (from the `linkedin/` media folder).
- **Description** (paste this):

```
A student project: relive real stock market crashes (1973, 1987, 2000, 2008, 2011, 2020, 2022) one trading day at a time with $10,000 and real prices.

Under the game is a backtesting engine:
• No look-ahead: a decision at the close earns the next day's return.
• Realistic accounting: 5 bp per trade, cash earns the T-bill rate, S&P dividends included.
• 76 years of daily S&P 500 data plus the Nasdaq, from Yahoo Finance, FRED and Robert Shiller.

You race buy-and-hold and a trend + volatility-targeting strategy of the kind funds use. At the end, a permutation test against 2,000 random traders making the same number of trades tells you whether your timing was skill or luck.

After each game you get a move-by-move review of what every trade made or cost you, plus a link that challenges a friend to beat your score on the same crash. New secret crash every day. Tested in CI against real historic days (Black Monday −20.47%).

JavaScript · Canvas · Python · Statistics
```

## 5. The media kit

All of it comes from one real run of the game (2008, sold at Lehman, bought back March 2009,
+15.3% vs the market's −22.0%). Every number on it is what the game produced.

- `crash-replay-motion.mp4`: 50-second gameplay video, 1080x1350 (4:5, the LinkedIn feed size).
  Upload it as a native video, not a link. Add captions off; the text is burned in.
- `crash-replay-carousel.pdf`: 8 slides. Post it with **Add a document** so it swipes.
  Slides 1-8 are also PNGs if you want single images.

Post text to go with the carousel (link goes in the first comment):

```
Would you have sold in 2008?

I built a game that drops you into real stock market crashes with $10,000 and real daily prices. You decide one trading day at a time while the headlines hit.

My run: sold the day Lehman failed, bought back in March 2009. +15.3% while the market lost 22%.

Then the game asked the honest question: skill or luck? It replays the crash for 2,000 random traders making the same 2 trades. I beat 92% of them. p ≈ 0.08, so luck can't be ruled out.

7 real crashes (1973 to 2022), a new secret one every day, and a challenge link to send a friend.

Student project, open source. Link in the comments. Beat my score.
```
